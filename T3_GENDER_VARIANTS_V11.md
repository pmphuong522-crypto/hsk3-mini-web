# T3 gender-pronoun pilot expansion — V11

## Change
Added two reviewed, audio-ambiguous T3 example IDs to the narrow allowlist:
- H3-119-ex01 — 他几乎不喝可乐，因为喝太多可乐对身体不好。
- H3-121-ex01 — 他以前不喜欢运动，现在每天都运动，身体越来越健康了。

For these examples, the grader accepts the canonical sentence and a direct 他 ↔ 她 substitution. It does not accept 它, arbitrary homophones, or omissions/additions elsewhere in the sentence. Other examples remain strict unless they are explicitly listed in `T3_GENDER_VARIANTS_PILOT_V1.md`.

## User-reported browser verification
The user confirmed `再等一会儿吧，他可能快到了。` accepts the `她` variant. They then reported the two examples above were still graded wrong when changing 他 to 她; those two IDs are now allowlisted.
